# Offline world map

`worldmap.bin` is the map of the location picker (Settings, Light, Location, "Pick on the map"). It is generated by
`scripts/gen_world_map.py` from two sources:

- Natural Earth (land and boundary lines at 1:50m), public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/
- GeoNames `cities5000` (names, places and populations of the cities with more than 5000 inhabitants), licensed
  under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0: https://www.geonames.org and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  The attribution is also shown in the location picker.

Regenerate with `python3 scripts/gen_world_map.py` from `app/` (needs internet once; the app never does). The file
format is described at the top of `Sources/IntermezzoKit/WorldMap.swift`.
