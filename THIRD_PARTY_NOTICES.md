## Synthesised sounds (`app/Support/tracks/*.wav`)

`bowl.wav`, `ocean.wav`, `pad.wav` and `rain.wav` are generated by `app/tools/gen_tracks.py` with no samples. They are covered by the project licence.

## Map data (`app/Support/worldmap/worldmap.bin`)

The offline map of the location picker is generated by `app/scripts/gen_world_map.py` from:

- [Natural Earth](https://www.naturalearthdata.com) land and boundary lines (public domain).
- [GeoNames](https://www.geonames.org) `cities5000`: names, places and populations of cities with more than 5000 inhabitants, licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The data was simplified and converted to a compact binary file.

## Services and integrations

- Weather data: [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) (CC BY 4.0), used only when the weather effect is on.
- [BetterDisplay](https://github.com/waydabber/BetterDisplay) is an optional, separately installed app. Intermezzo talks to it through its public notification interface and bundles none of its code.
