# Code of conduct

Intermezzo is a small personal project. I want it to be a friendly place to ask questions, report problems and suggest improvements. This code of conduct is short on purpose; it is written in the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).

## What I ask of everyone

- Be kind and patient. Assume good faith, and remember that people have different experience and a different first language.
- Keep criticism about the work, not the person. Be specific and constructive.
- No harassment, insults, discrimination, personal attacks or unwelcome sexual attention, in any form.
- Do not publish other people's private information, such as an address or email, without their permission.
- Stay on topic. Issues and pull requests are for this project.

## Where it applies

In the issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space of this repository, and when someone represents the project in public.

## If something goes wrong

If you see or experience behaviour that breaks these rules, report it. Comments, issues and pull requests that break them may be hidden, edited or removed, and a person who keeps breaking them may be blocked from the repository.

To report a problem, open an issue titled "Conduct" **without details** and ask for a private way to talk (or use GitHub's "Report content" on the item itself). Reports are handled promptly and privately, and the person who reports is not exposed.
